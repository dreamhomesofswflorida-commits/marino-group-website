How we work

Four steps from first call to final install.

  1. Request a quote

    Call, text or send the form. We set up a free consultation at your home to talk through what you want.

  2. Get your estimate

    You receive a clear, written estimate with the design, materials and price laid out. No hidden fees.

  3. We build it

    Your cabinets and woodwork are built to your measurements, style and finish.

  4. Installation

    Our team installs everything, cleans up, and walks the finished work with you.

About us

A family business that treats your home like ours.

Wooden Concept Renovations is a family-owned company in Naples, FL. Kitchens are our specialty, and we also build bathrooms, closets and custom wood features.

We measure carefully, keep you updated, and do not call a job finished until you are happy with it. When we are done, we ask for one thing: an honest Google review.
