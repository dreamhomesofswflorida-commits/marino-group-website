Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy covers the Marino Group website (marinogroupswfl.com) and the Marino Group app for iPhone and Android. Marino Group is a real estate team brokered by Waterfront Realty Group, Inc., serving Southwest Florida.

What we collect

We only collect what you choose to send us. When you fill in a form, such as a message, a home value request or a showing request, we receive:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • A property address, if you enter one
  • The message you write

The app does not require an account. It does not read your contacts, photos or location, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

How we use it

We use your information only to answer you: to call, text or email you back about buying, selling, renting or building a home. We do not sell your information, and we do not share it with advertisers.

Services we use

  • Form messages are delivered to our team's email by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co).
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.
  • Home listings come from the Southwest Florida MLS. Search pages may be provided by our MLS search provider, which has its own privacy policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages in our team email for as long as needed to help you, and as required by Florida real estate record rules. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete the information you sent us, email dhomeswfl@gmail.com or call (239) 776-2393.

Children

Our website and app are for adults looking for homes. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.
