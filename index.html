Our six-step plan to sell your home.

  1. Selling consultation

    We learn your goals, timing, and what matters most, then build a plan around them.

  2. Pricing strategy

    We price from current market conditions and real comparable sales, so your home sells for the most it can.

  3. Marketing strategy

    Professional photos, MLS, social media, and our agents' buyer network, in English, Spanish, and Russian.

  4. Preparing your home

    Decluttering, curb appeal, and small fixes that make the best first impression.

  5. Negotiating the contract

    We review every offer with you: price, deposit, financing, inspections, and closing date.

  6. Managing to closing

    We handle the timeline from accepted offer to keys and keep you updated the way you prefer.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Can't find your answer? Call or text (239) 776-2393.

How do I list my home with Marino Group?

Call, text, or fill out the home-value form. We schedule a free consultation, show you what similar homes sold for, and agree on a plan before anything goes on the market.

What areas do you serve?

All of Southwest Florida: Collier, Lee, and Charlotte counties, including Naples, Golden Gate Estates, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, Estero, Fort Myers, Lehigh Acres, Cape Coral, and Port Charlotte.

How do you price my home?

We compare recent sales of similar homes nearby, adjust for condition, size, and features, and factor in current interest rates and buyer demand.

Can you help me get a mortgage?

Yes. We connect you with local lenders who can pre-approve you, so your offers are taken seriously.

Can I build a new home with you?

Yes. Choose a model, and we'll help you with the lot, the financing, and the build through our licensed builder partner.

What languages do you speak?

Our team speaks English, Spanish, and Russian, so you can work with us in the language you are most comfortable with.
