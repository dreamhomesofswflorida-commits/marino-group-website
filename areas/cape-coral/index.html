Living in Cape Coral

Cape Coral is famous for its huge canal system, one of the largest in the world. Many homes have a dock in the backyard, and saltwater canals give boaters access to the Gulf of Mexico.

Lifestyle

Why people love Cape Coral

  • Boating from your backyard on many canal homes
  • Fishing, kayaking and waterfront dining
  • A wide range of prices, from starter homes to luxury waterfront
  • Close to Fort Myers and the beaches
Homes

What you'll find

Single-family canal homes (saltwater and freshwater), dry-lot homes, new construction and vacant lots.

Check before you buy

  • Saltwater or freshwater canal, and whether it has Gulf access
  • Bridges or locks between the home and the Gulf, and their clearance for your boat
  • Seawall condition
  • City water and sewer, or any pending utility assessment
