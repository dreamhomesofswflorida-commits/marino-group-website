Questions

Before you buy

Do I need a real estate license?

No. Court foreclosure and tax deed sales in Florida are open to the public. The course explains what you need to register and bid.

How do I get the lessons?

Right after checkout you get an email with your access link. Keep it, because it is how you log back in.

Is the course in Spanish?

Yes. Every lesson, caption, checklist and the calculator come in both English and Spanish. Switch languages anytime. / Sí, todo el curso está en inglés y en español.

Is this only for Collier County?

The examples are from Collier County. The process is similar in most Florida counties, but each Clerk has its own rules and deadlines.

Do you buy at these sales too?

Yes. Jorge Marino LLC invests in Collier County foreclosures and tax deeds. We will never bid on a property that a Coaching student brings to us for review.

Do I have to use your real estate team?

No. Marino Group, brokered by Waterfront Realty Group, Inc., may represent students who choose to hire us to buy or sell, but it is never required to take or finish the course.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. If the course is not for you, email within 14 days of purchase for a full refund.
